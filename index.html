THE RESEARCHER

My research asks how AI systems can earn trust through evidence, diagnostics, and honest evaluation. In clinical NLP, that means every extracted claim should remain traceable to its source. In RAG, verification effort should match query complexity. In reinforcement learning, internal training signals can reveal failure before reward makes it visible. I am now extending this thread toward AI safety, agent evaluation, robustness, and scalable oversight.
